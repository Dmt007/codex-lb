## Why

Company gateway configuration is hidden in Advanced Settings. Operators want to add and control it beside their existing accounts.

## What Changes

- Render the existing model-source management controls in the Accounts list column.
- Keep one shared source store and the existing settings controls.
- Use a compact layout and respect source administration permissions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `model-source-routing`: source management is available beside accounts.

## Impact

Accounts page and shared model-source dashboard component; no new backend settings or credentials store.
